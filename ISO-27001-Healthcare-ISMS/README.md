# ISO 27001 ISMS Implementation: Healthcare Provider Case Study

Note: This is a training case study completed as part of a GRC course. The organisation and scenario are fictional.

## Scenario
A growing healthcare provider with three clinics had no structured information security management system (ISMS). I acted as a junior GRC consultant preparing the organisation for ISO/IEC 27001 certification.

## What I did
- Defined the ISMS scope and security objectives
- Carried out a gap analysis against ISO/IEC 27001 requirements
- Recommended Annex A controls with justifications.
- Built an internal audit checklist
- Wrote a PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act) improvement plan
- Prioritised recommendations with a 6 to 9 month certification timeline

## Key findings
- No defined ISMS scope or complete policy set
- No asset inventory, risk register or Statement of Applicability
- No regular security awareness training or supplier security evaluation
